A family of stone craftsmen, working in Rajasthan

Kom Handicrafts Manufacturing & Trading Company was established in 2007 by Radha Mohan Saini in Sikandara, Dausa. We work with marble, granite and other natural stone, turning raw blocks into handicrafts, home and garden décor and made-to-order pieces.

A team of about 40 people handles cutting, carving, polishing and finishing under one roof. Every piece is shaped for the way it will be used, and customized production is available for projects with their own drawings, sizes or finishes.
